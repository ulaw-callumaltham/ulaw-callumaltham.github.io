Tutorial

A for loop repeats code once for each item in a sequence. It is the natural choice when you know how many times you want to repeat.

for letter in "Python":
    print(letter)

On each pass, the variable letter holds the next character.


The range() Function

range() produces a sequence of numbers to loop over:

  • range(5) gives 0, 1, 2, 3, 4 (it stops before 5)
  • range(1, 6) gives 1, 2, 3, 4, 5
  • range(0, 11, 2) gives 0, 2, 4, 6, 8, 10 (the third value is the step)
for i in range(1, 4):
    print(f"Round {i}")

while or for?

Use a for loop when you know how many repetitions you need, or are working through a sequence. Use a while loop when you are repeating until something happens, such as the user typing the right answer.
