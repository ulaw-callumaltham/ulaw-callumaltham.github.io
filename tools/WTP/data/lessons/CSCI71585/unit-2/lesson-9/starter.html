Why Accessibility Matters

Published on 12 October 2026

Millions of people in the UK have a disability, and many of them use assistive technology to browse the web. If a website is not built with them in mind, they may not be able to use it at all.

Who Benefits?

Accessibility helps far more people than you might expect: someone with a broken arm using a keyboard, someone reading in bright sunlight, or someone on a slow connection. Good accessibility is simply good design.

Where to Start

The easiest place to start is with the HTML itself. Use the right element for each piece of content, write useful alt text for images, and make sure the headings form a clear outline.
