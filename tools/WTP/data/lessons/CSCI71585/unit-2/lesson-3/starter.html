Simple Tomato Soup

A quick, warming soup made from cupboard ingredients. Serves 4 and takes about 30 minutes.

Ingredients

- 1 tablespoon olive oil
- 1 onion, chopped
- 2 cloves of garlic, crushed
- 2 tins of chopped tomatoes
- 500ml vegetable stock
- Salt and pepper

Method

1. Heat the oil in a large pan over a medium heat.
2. Add the onion and cook for 5 minutes until soft.
3. Add the tomatoes and stock, and bring to the boil.
4. Reduce the heat and simmer for 15 minutes.
5. Blend the soup until smooth.
6. Season with salt and pepper, and serve.
